FAQ

Good questions.

Is it safe? Does it see my data?

It runs inside your browser tab using your own Zepto session. There is no server and no analytics. Saved items and price history stay in your browser.

Why do I get no results at first?

Zepto shows products only for a delivery location. Open zepto.com, choose your location, then scan again.

Is it official?

No. It is an independent tool, not affiliated with Zepto. It only reads what your account can already see.

Will it keep working?

Zepto can change its internal APIs at any time. The parser reads responses loosely to survive small changes, and the tool tells you when a scan returns nothing.

How does price-drop tracking work?

After each scan the tool remembers every price in your browser. Next time, new products are labelled New and cheaper ones Price drop.

Where does the data come from?

Live from Zepto, inside your own logged-in tab and for your delivery address. Nothing is stored on any server; saved items and price history stay in your browser.
